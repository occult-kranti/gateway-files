01 The Dossier What these files are
and where they came from

In June 1983, a U.S. Army intelligence officer sat down at Fort Meade and wrote a 29-page memo to his commander. Its subject was not a weapon system or a Soviet unit. It was a consciousness-training program run by a small institute in the Virginia foothills — a program built on headphones, humming, and the claim that the human mind can leave the body, see distant places, and slip outside of time. The memo became the most famous document in the CIA's FOIA Reading Room.

Three documents make up the core "Gateway files." Everything on this site is built from them, and every claim carries a page reference back to the scans:

DocumentWhat it containsOriginal
Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process (1983) LTC Wayne McDonnell's theoretical assessment for U.S. Army INSCOM: hypnosis, meditation and biofeedback mechanics; Hemi-Sync; a holographic-universe model of consciousness; the full Gateway method; out-of-body protocols; and a proposed 12-step Army exploitation plan. CIA scan ↗
Gateway Intermediate Workbook (1977) The Monroe Institute's actual exercise manual given to Gateway participants: the affirmations, the Energy Bar Tool, the Living Body Map, resonant energy balloon, remote viewing procedure, Focus 10/12 drills, and waking-life "daily tools." CIA scan ↗

The record, in order

Robert Monroe, a Virginia radio executive, begins reporting involuntary out-of-body experiences; he spends the next decade audio-engineering ways to reproduce them.

1971

Monroe publishes Journeys Out of the Body — later cited as reference #5 in the Army report's own bibliography.

1972

Physicists Hal Puthoff and Russell Targ begin remote-viewing research at SRI under intelligence-community funding — the start of what becomes GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK and finally STAR GATE.

1970s

Monroe founds the Monroe Institute of Applied Sciences (Faber/Afton, VA) and patents "Hemi-Sync" — binaural-beat audio for synchronizing the brain's hemispheres.

1977

The Gateway Intermediate Workbook is printed for participants of the Gateway Program's first session — the document the CIA later releases as RDP96-00788R001700210023-7.

9 June 1983

1991–1995

1995

The American Institutes for Research evaluation (statistician Jessica Utts vs. skeptic Ray Hyman) delivers a split verdict; Congress defunds the program; the CIA begins declassifying the files.

Analyst's note The 1983 report is an assessment written by a believer-in-progress: McDonnell took the course, read the physics popularizations of his day, and built the best rational scaffold he could. It is a primary historical source — not a peer-reviewed validation. Chapter 09 separates the report's claims from what mainstream science supports.
02 The Model of Reality §5–§27 · the physics
the report builds

Before teaching a single technique, McDonnell spends twenty pages building a machine in words: a model in which consciousness is energy, the universe is a hologram, and the Gateway tapes are a tuning device. This chapter walks that machine piece by piece — in the report's own terms.

2.1 — Hemi-Sync and the frequency following response

Monroe Institute trainer Melissa Jager defines Hemi-Sync as "a state of consciousness defined when the EEG patterns of both hemispheres are simultaneously equal in amplitude and frequency." The engineering trick that produces it is the frequency following response (FFR): play two slightly different audible tones, one per ear, and the brain "hears" neither — it entrains to the difference, the beat frequency. Feed it a 4 Hz beat and the brain begins producing coherent 4 Hz-band output, at marginal, almost subliminal volume. §5–6 · CIA p.7 §7 · p.8

Exhibit A — Frequency Following Responseafter report §7; values illustrativeSOURCE: CIA SCAN p.8 ↗

Jager's metaphor, quoted at length in the report: the ordinary mind is a lamp — energy radiated chaotically in all directions. Under Hemi-Sync it becomes a laser — the same energy, coherent in frequency and amplitude, "billions of times" more concentrated per unit area. Gateway's wager is that once brain output is coherent, it can be accelerated to "ever higher vibrational levels" and synchronized with "more sophisticated and rarified energy levels in the universe." §6 · CIA p.7

2.2 — The body as a 7 Hz oscillator

From inventor-mystic Itzhak Bentov's biomedical model, the report takes its central physiological claim. Deep relaxation (whether by meditation, the tapes, or biofeedback) slows and softens the heartbeat until the "bifurcation echo" — the pressure pulse that normally rebounds where the aorta forks toward the legs and collides with the next pulse — dies away. The body's fluid systems then fall into a single standing wave: the whole organism vibrating coherently at about 7–7.5 cycles per second, roughly three times the heart's normal sound amplitude, propagating "in consonance with the electrostatic field of the earth." §8–10 · CIA p.9

Exhibit B — Bifurcation echo → planetary-scale resonanceBentov's model as quoted in the reportSOURCE: CIA SCAN p.9 ↗

"This is occurring at a very long wavelength of about 40,000 Km, or just about the perimeter of the planet. In other words, the signal from the movement of our bodies will travel around the world in about one seventh of a second through the electrostatic field in which we are imbedded. Such a long wavelength knows no obstacles… It is the ideal medium for conveying a telepathic signal."

— Bentov, quoted in §8, Role of Resonance (CIA scan p.9) · note: 7 Hz at light speed ≈ 43,000 km, matching the Schumann resonance band (~7.83 Hz) — the real physics the model leans on

The report then chains three consequences. Brain stimulation (§9): the sustained resonance rising up into the head is equated with the Kundalini phenomenon of yoga, per Lee Sannella's Kundalini — Psychosis or Transcendence? Energy entrainment (§10): resonating with Earth's field homogenizes the body's own field with its surroundings, so "the seat of consciousness" can begin to move into the environment — and the body becomes "a surprisingly powerful carrier wave" for mind-to-mind signaling. Consciousness and energy (§11): consciousness is recast as organized energy interacting with the universal field. §9–10 · p.9 §11 · p.10

2.3 — The holographic mind

The next layer borrows neuroscientist Karl Pribram and physicist David Bohm: the universe is "one gigantic hologram of unbelievable complexity," and the human mind is also a hologram that reads the universal one by energy exchange. Because a hologram's every fragment encodes the whole image, "the part encodes the whole" — mind can in principle access the totality. What you can read depends on your frequency and amplitude: change them and the mind projects a different "holographic energy matrix" to intercept meaning directly from the universe's transmissions. §12–13 · p.10–11 §14 · p.11

Exhibit C — Holographic encodingrecreation of the report's §12–13 argumentSOURCE: CIA SCAN p.11 ↗

Two further pieces complete the cognitive machine. Brain in phase (§15): consciousness arises when the mind's hologram and the universal hologram resonate in phase; Exhibit 2 of the report assigns the left hemisphere the role of software that reduces right-hemisphere input to "verbal symbols and concepts," and the right hemisphere the reduction of "three dimensional holographic image to two dimensional go/no go focus." Self cognition (§17): humans know that they know because the mind duplicates part of its own hologram, projects it, perceives the projection, and measures the difference against memory. §15–17 · p.12 Exhibit 2 · p.13

2.4 — Time-space, Planck "click-outs," and the Absolute

Now "the fun begins" (McDonnell's words). Time is a measure of energy in motion; energy confined in a vibratory pattern gets location — space. Unconfined energy is infinite, unmoving, outside both: the Absolute. An oscillating wave must pass through points of rest to reverse direction — and in that infinitesimal instant, when its speed drops below Planck's distance (10⁻³³ cm per instant), energy "clicks out" of time-space and joins infinity. Human consciousness, driven to sufficiently high frequency by Hemi-Sync, clicks out so often that the click-outs approach continuity: a portion of consciousness takes up station "between time-space and the Absolute." §18–20 · p.14 §21 · p.15 Exhibit 3 · p.16

2.5 — From Big Bang to torus

The cosmology is pure Bentov: the universe as an immense self-contained spiral — a torus or "cosmic egg." Matter jets from a compressed nucleus through a white hole, evolves as it travels around the ovoid shell (this movement is time), and re-enters through a black hole to be reabsorbed. Our galaxy sits near the top of the egg, where the stream turns back on itself — offered as an explanation for why northern galaxies recede faster. Layered over the egg: the Absolute, sustaining the radiating nucleus. Consciousness that reaches the Absolute's "rest" state perceives the whole pattern — past, present, future — at once: the theoretical license for Focus 15 and Focus 21. §24 · p.18 §25–27 · p.20

Exhibit E — The cosmic egg / torus (recreation of report Exhibit 4–5)Bentov, Stalking the Wild Pendulum; torus after Purce, The Mystic SpiralSOURCE: CIA SCAN p.19 ↗ · p.21 ↗
Analyst's note Pieces of this chapter gesture at real science — binaural beats and the FFR exist; ~7.83 Hz is the Schumann fundamental; Pribram and Bohm seriously proposed holographic models; Planck length is real. But the report welds them into claims (telepathic carrier waves, consciousness leaving time-space) that no experiment in the declassified record validates. Treat the model as the report's working hypothesis, exactly as McDonnell framed it.

04 The Protocols §28–§33 + Workbook
every method, step by step

This is the operational heart of the files: every exercise the documents teach, reconstructed as protocols. The sequence matters — Gateway is a ladder, and each rung is drilled before the next is introduced. Section §28 of the report gives the induction order; the 1977 Workbook gives the field procedures.

Phase 0 — Induction (every session opens this way)

P-01Energy Conversion BoxPrep · C-1

The first device taught. Visualize a box with a heavy lid; mentally place inside it every worry, distraction, and "extraneous concern" of the day; close the lid and set the box aside. Its function is isolation — quarantining the noise of ordinary life before the descent begins.

P-02Resonant TuningPrep · C-1

A long, monotonous hum, voiced with the chorus on the tape — "a single tone… that sets up a feeling of vibration particularly in the head." The hum is not ritual; in the report's mechanics it is the first physical push toward bodily resonance (see Exhibit B).

SOURCE:§28 · CIA p.22

Repeated silently with the tape. The Workbook prints two forms; the first begins: "I am more than my physical body. The second affirmation gates all contact: "only to those whose knowledge, wisdom, development and experience is equal or greater than my own… at all other times, it will remain closed."

P-04Hemi-Sync Exposure → Focus 10Descent

First contact with the binaural frequencies, then progressive whole-body relaxation as the tape adds "pink and white noise" layers to push the body to the threshold of sleep while the right hemisphere stays alert. The Workbook's drill for doing it unaided:

  1. Lie or sit; relax as deeply as you can, "drifting down."
  2. When you can drift no further, exhale and say "two." Wait for a further sink.
  3. Continue "three," "four"… to "ten." Each number should deepen the state.
  4. Dissatisfied with the depth at ten? Restart from five, or two.
  5. To return: count slowly 10 → 1. Emergency return: move any physical body part (finger, foot, tongue).

P-05Resonant Energy Balloon (REBAL)Focus 10

The signature Gateway device: an energy flow blown out with the breath until it encloses you in a floating, luminous sphere. The report notes it mirrors the cosmic egg — and assigns it a protective function "against conscious entities possessing lower energy levels."

  1. In Focus 10, inhale deeply, drawing Energy up from all parts of the body; hold ~10 counts.
  2. Exhale down to the toes, "as though softly blowing out a candle." Repeat 8–10 times, rounding the flow's angle each time to build the balloon.
  3. Switch direction: inhale from the right, exhale left, letting the flow go "rounder" until a ball forms.
  4. Fill the sphere's spaces with bright light so it grows lighter and floats.
  5. To close: let the REBAL move inward and reabsorb into you.

The universal implement — the report calls it the tool for concentrating and directing one's energy; the Workbook makes it the first thing you build.

P-13Patterning (the "manifestation" protocol)Focus 12

The report's most quoted method. Consciousness is energy; thought patterns are energy patterns; energy patterns are, in the model's logic, seeds of reality.

The report's most-read section, and its most hedged: the Institute "stresses that the Gateway program was not established solely" for OBEs and guarantees nothing. One tape of many addresses it, using beta-range signals of "around 2877.3 CPS" per Monroe. When separation feels imminent, the Workbook's five sanctioned exit drills, performed inside REBAL:

  1. Roll out — like a log rolling in water.
  2. Rise rigid — going up like a stiff pole.
  3. Back away from the body.

05 Remote Viewing & the Army §33, §38 · why an
intelligence officer cared

McDonnell did not write a spirituality manual. His tasking was "mechanics and ultimate practicality" — could INSCOM use this? The report's final third turns from cosmology to tradecraft: how information collection actually works in altered states, why it distorts, and a twelve-step plan to operationalize it.

5.2 — Why the data distorts (§33)

The report is unusually sober about failure modes. In an out-of-body information-gathering state, the viewer may receive not only the target's present pattern but also (1) residual energy patterns of past people and events at the same site; (2) pure thought forms — since thoughts are energy patterns too, they "mingle with physical reality and are not easily differentiated"; and (3) pseudoscopic inversions, the hologram viewed inside-out or backwards. §33 · CIA p.26

5.3 — The fix: three viewers, three time-streams (Conclusion, step H)

McDonnell's engineering answer is triangulation across dimensions. Send three people out-of-body together: one views the target here in time-space; one at Focus 15 as it slips into the immediate past; one at Focus 21 as it slips from the immediate future. Debrief all three, compare. If they separate together in the same environment, "their consciousness energy systems should resonate in sympathetic oscillation." Conclusion H · CIA p.29

5.4 — The accelerated exploitation plan (Conclusion A–L)

The report's final page is a twelve-step program for making Gateway usable "within more manageable limits" across an organization. Abridged verbatim:

  1. A. Hemi-Sync tapes for enhanced brain focus and hemisphere synchronization.
  2. B. Add strong REM-sleep frequencies for left-brain quiescence and deep physical relaxation.
  3. C. Hypnotic suggestion enabling a deep autohypnotic state at will.
  4. D. Autohypnotic suggestion for accelerated progress through the Focus 12 exercises.
  5. E. Repeat A–B under suggestion that an out-of-body movement will occur and be remembered.
  6. F. Repeat E toward voluntary, controlled OBE sustained beyond REM.
  7. G. Approach Focus 15 and 21 objectives from the out-of-body perspective.
  8. H. Multi-focus triangulation (above) against terrestrial information targets.
  9. I. Pursue full self-knowledge in all participants to keep observation objective and remove energy blockages.
  10. J. Be intellectually prepared for "possible encounters with intelligent, non-corporal energy forms" beyond time-space.
  11. K. Groups in Focus 12 unite altered consciousness to build holographic patterns shielding sensitive areas "to repulse possible unwanted out-of-body presences."
  12. L. Advanced participants build "holographic patterns of successful attainment" to pull colleagues up the ladder faster.

5.5 — How the story ends in the official record

Steps K and J never got their field trial as written. The parent program — by then STAR GATE — was referred in 1995 to the American Institutes for Research. Statistician Jessica Utts judged the lab evidence for anomalous cognition statistically significant; psychologist Ray Hyman judged the database methodologically flawed and the effects non-replicable as intelligence. The review's practical conclusion: remote viewing had not produced actionable intelligence, and the program was terminated and declassified. CIA STAR GATE collection ↗ 1993 overview ↗

06 The Missing Page 25 restored 2021
read it here

For eighteen years the CIA's public copy of the report jumped from printed page 24 straight to 26. The gap fell mid-sentence in §34, "Belief System Considerations." Absence bred legend: the page was said to contain the real secret — the simulation, the escape instructions, the thing they didn't want you to see.

In April 2021, Vice published the page, located in the Monroe Institute's own copy of the report. The scan now circulates in complete form (Wikimedia Commons, 30 pages), and the local archive includes an annotated transcript with the page restored. The reveal: no redacted smoking gun — instead, the report's most openly mystical page, where an Army lieutenant colonel maps his holographic cosmology onto Buddhism, Hinduism, Kabbalah and Christian trinitarian theology, then explains why that matters for intelligence work.

Restored · Printed page 25

The Gateway report sits at a strange three-way junction: a genuine military artifact, a sincere summary of 1970s consciousness speculation, and a modern internet scripture. A disciplined reading keeps the three separate.
