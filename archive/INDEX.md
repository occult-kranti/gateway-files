Discovery, Threshold, Freedom, Adventure, Exploring, Prospecting, Voyager, plus Wave
VIII Union) are commercial audio programs — many hours of Hemi-Sync® recordings. They
are not copied here (size + copyright). Official sources: https://hemi-sync.com and
https://www.monroeinstitute.org.
