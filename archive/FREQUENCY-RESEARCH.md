# FREQUENCY & HEALING — Research Dossier (compiled 2026-10-14)

Grading convention: **A** replicated physics/evidence · **B** some human/animal RCT evidence ·
**C** plausible, indirect, or contested · **D** folklore/marketing. Dual grade `A/D` = exact
arithmetic, symbolic meaning. Every claim keeps its numbers and its source.

**Physics honesty box.** Sound is a pressure wave, not photons; "frequency healing by
resonance" claims must survive E=hf and Q: cells are damped bags of water (Q≈1), so weak
audio cannot selectively "shatter" anything. What sound *can* do: mask (sleep), pace
attention/arousal (relaxation), drive rhythmic neural responses when loud and periodic
(ASSR/40 Hz GENUS), and vibrate tissue mechanically (ultrasound, vibroacoustics).

## 4. What this means for the Gateway files

- The report's FFR mechanism is real physiology; the strong version ("synchronize, then
  access the holographic universe") is not supported — 2023 SOTA leaves even basic
  entrainment unsettled.
- The strongest frequency-healing science alive today (40 Hz GENUS) works by *rhythmic
  sensory driving* at clinical doses (1 hr/day, months), not by mystical resonance — and
  even it awaits phase III confirmation.
- The same government that explored sound for *consciousness expansion* (Gateway 1983)
  also explored it for *crowd control and interrogation* (LRAD, MEDUSA, Gitmo). The
  physics doesn't care which story you tell about it; the ethics are in the use.
