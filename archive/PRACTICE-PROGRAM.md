## Universal session protocol (every sitting, ~10 min setup)

1. Stereo headphones, no noise-reduction/Dolby processing.
2. Darkened, undisturbed room for ~45 min; phone off.
3. Wait ≥1 h after eating; use the bathroom; loosen clothing, remove glasses.
4. No alcohol, drugs, or excessive caffeine; no driving ever; not with epilepsy;
   doctor's sign-off if on psychotropic medication (official manual cautions).
5. Posture: lying down or sitting with head supported — find your preference.
6. Energy Conversion Box → resonant hum → Gateway Affirmation → REBAL.
7. **Always hear the track to the end** — the closing frequencies return you to C1.
   If interrupted, skip to the end and play the return section.
8. Journal 2–3 lines immediately after (the Workbook's Game Plan form).

## The 8-week ramp

- **Weeks 1–2 — Wave I only.** One track per day, in order, each to completion.
  Goal: reach F10 reliably by count-down without the tape.
- **Weeks 3–4 — Wave II.** Add F12; begin EBT and Color Breathing; start the journal grid.
- **Weeks 5–6 — Wave III.** Remote Viewing, Five Questions, Energy Food; first
  First-Stage-Separation attempts on weekend mornings.
- **Weeks 7–8 — Wave IV.** Patterning + Free Flow 12. Then decide: consolidate
  F12 skills for another month (recommended) or proceed to Wave V/VI.
- Waves V–VIII: one wave per month minimum; these are advanced states.
