# READING LIST — Books & Sources Behind the Gateway Files

These books are copyrighted and therefore linked, not copied. Where a legal free/
borrow copy exists (Internet Archive controlled lending), that is noted.

## The report's own bibliography (as printed in CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5)

1. Itzhak Bentov, *Stalking the Wild Pendulum*, E.P. Dutton, 1977 — the biomedical
   model (body as coherent oscillator, 7–7.5 Hz resonance, cosmic egg) that
   McDonnell leans on hardest.
2. Marilyn Ferguson, "Karl Pribram's Changing Reality," *Human Behavior*, May 1978.
3. John Gliedman, "Einstein Against the Odds: The Great Quantum Debate," *Science
   Digest*, June 1983.
4. Melissa Jager, monograph "The Lamp Turned Laser," Monroe Institute of Applied
   Sciences (undated) — the lamp-vs-laser coherence metaphor; not commercially
   published.
5. Robert A. Monroe, *Journeys Out of the Body*, Doubleday, 1971 — the founder's
   first-person OBE account. (Borrow: Internet Archive controlled lending.)
6. Jill Purce, *The Mystic Spiral*, Thames & Hudson, 1980 — source of the torus
   figure in Exhibit 4.
7. Lee Sannella, M.D., *Kundalini — Psychosis or Transcendence?*, Henry S. Dakin,
   1976 — basis for the "brain stimulation"/kundalini passage.
8. Pat Stone, "Altered States of Consciousness," *The Mother Earth News*,
   March/April 1983 — source of the Monroe "2877.3 CPS" quote.
9. Charles T. Tart, *Altered States of Consciousness*, Wiley, 1969.

## Remote viewing / STAR GATE context

- Harold Puthoff & Russell Targ, *Mind-Reach* (1977) — SRI remote-viewing research.
- Russell Targ & Harold Puthoff, *The Race for the Mind* / Targ, *The Reality of ESP* (2012).
- Joseph McMoneagle, *Mind Trek* (1993) and *Remote Viewing Secrets* (2000).
- Lyn Buchanan, *The Seventh Sense* (2003) — CRV trainer's account.
- Paul H. Smith, *Reading the Enemy's Mind* (2005).
- Ingo Swann, *Penetration* (1998).
- David Morehouse, *Psychic Warrior* (1996).
- Annie Jacobsen, *Phenomena* (2017) — journalist's history of the government's
  psychic programs.
- American Institutes for Research, *An Evaluation of Remote Viewing: Research and
  Applications* (1995) — the external review (Utts vs. Hyman) whose mixed verdict
  ended the program. Summary: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/ (STAR GATE collection).

## Science-skeptical grounding

- Ray Hyman, "Evaluation of the Military's Twenty-Year Program on Psychic Spying,"
  *Skeptical Inquirer* 20(2), 1996.
- Jessica Utts, "An Assessment of the Evidence for Psychic Functioning," same AIR
  review — the pro-evidence counterweight.
- Karl Pribram, *Languages of the Brain* (1971); David Bohm, *Wholeness and the
  Implicate Order* (1980) — the real holographic-mind physics the report popularizes.
